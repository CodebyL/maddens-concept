After an accident

Just got hit? Here's what to do next.

A short checklist so nothing gets missed, whether it was a fender bender or someone hit your parked car and drove off.

  1. Make sure everyone is okayThen move to a safe spot if the car can be driven.
  2. Take photosBoth cars, the plates, the street and every dent, close up and from a step back.
  3. Swap informationName, phone, insurance and plate number. If nobody is there, file a police report.
  4. Open a claimCall your insurance company and write down the claim number.
  5. Send us the photosUse the photo estimate below and we will tell you what we see.
  6. Drop it offWe work with your adjuster and call you when it's ready.

Good to know: in New York, your insurance company can't make you use a particular body shop. You choose who fixes your car.
